# Product Vision

## Vision

Build a personal operating system that helps people:

1. Know what matters.
2. Plan ahead.
3. Decide what to do next.
4. Focus on meaningful work.
5. Measure real progress.
6. Learn from their behavior.
7. Improve their planning over time.

## Problem

Most productivity tools primarily help users store tasks.

They do not sufficiently help users decide:
- what matters
- what to work on now
- whether their plans are realistic
- whether they are actually progressing

## Product Promise

When the user opens the system, it should help answer:

1. What am I trying to achieve?
2. What matters today?
3. What should I do next?
4. Am I making progress?
5. What should I change?

## Non-goals

The system is not intended to become:
- a generic project-management platform
- a social network
- a heavily gamified productivity system
- a replacement for every calendar feature
